ABOUT THE HUB

A practical academic platform for pharmacy.

Pharma Research & Education Hub is designed as a free resource platform for pharmacy students, researchers, teachers and academic professionals.

The platform can host original educational material, research guides, templates, question banks, presentations, laboratory resources and links to legitimate scholarly resources. It is not a journal and does not issue ISSNs, DOIs or indexing claims.
